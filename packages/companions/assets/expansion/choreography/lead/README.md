# LEAD choreography

Sixteen authored drawings are exported as 256 × 384 transparent layered rigs.
The canvas uses top-left pixels and a shared foot baseline at y = 368.
Seated and kneeling drawings retain their physical head scale; they are not
expanded to standing height. Each rig has a body, an independent head and at
least one moving forearm/hand. Tablets remain beneath their authored fingers.

The four dance drawings provide the large changes in pose. Measured distal arm
joints add 1.5–3 degree accents without exposing missing torso artwork. Work
gestures are limited to 0.6–1.2 degrees. New side/back views use the real drawn
head; a shared empty facial atlas prevents front-facing mouth overlays.

Sources in `../../source/choreography-v1/` are immutable ImageGen outputs:

- `lead.png` — original four work views and four dance poses.
- `lead-more-v1.png` — back view, walks, kneeling, leaning and desk work.
- `lead-right-views-v2.png` — separately drawn right-facing work views.
- `lead-walk-phases-v3.png` — a right-facing contact step and passing step.

`walk-left` and `walk-right` identify gait phases, not camera directions. Both
face right with matched physical head scale and height. Earlier walking
iterations remain as source history and are excluded from runtime files.

The matching `lead-*-prompt.json` files retain the exact built-in
`image_gen.imagegen` prompts and references. No API/CLI fallback was used.

`spec.json` stores measured source rectangles, uniform scale, pivots and cut
polygons. Source pixels with alpha below 24 are cleared before interpolation;
exported pixels below alpha 8 are fully canonicalized, including hidden RGB.
Detached pixels at measured cut edges are transferred to the adjacent moving
layer instead of discarded. Joint underlap is restricted to four existing
edge pixels hidden beneath the moving layer. No exposed face or anatomy is
redrawn by the preparation script. `headLowerEdge` traces the anatomical
boundary beneath hair/skin; `headBodyPixels` records small confirmed garment
corners. Fabric returns to the body. Corrected rigs restrict joint underlap to
fully opaque occluded pixels, preserving exact neutral RGBA. Near-empty prop
layers are merged into their holding arm and removed from the runtime inventory.

`manifest.json` uses expansion-relative runtime paths. It lists 71 unique PNG
files, including one shared neutral facial atlas. Local QA is written to
`.local/choreography-qa/lead/`: light/dark contact sheets, registered/composed
drawings, both joint-limit previews and numerical composition differences.
Native renderer integration and final native visual verification are performed
by the parent task; these contact sheets verify asset preparation only.

Run `test_layers.py` with the same Python for ten regression tests. Immutable
`ownership-fixtures.json` stores confirmed fabric samples and retained face,
hair and green-iris pixels; the test also compares repaired compositions with
their registered source. The fixture is intentionally not regenerated by
`prepare.py`, so an incorrect future cut cannot silently update the expectation.
